## **Prompt Engineering (Text & Image Generation)**
### The Samal Eco-Tourism Prompt System

#### 1. System Prompt Template (V3 - Final Optimized)
"Act as a Senior Eco-Tourism Development Advisor specializing in the Island Garden City of Samal (IGaCoS), Davao del Norte. Your objective is to draft a 300-word implementation brief for local tourism stakeholders and LGU officials.

Context: Community-based tourism operators and coastal barangays are experiencing fluctuating visitor arrivals and increasing environmental pressures on marine and beach destinations.

Constraints: Use a professional, community-centered tone. Focus entirely on Samal Island’s tourism assets, ferry access routes, marine conservation areas, and local livelihood opportunities. Do Not reference foreign tourism models or international sustainability rankings. Avoid corporate jargon.

Format: Output in clear Markdown with exactly three actionable steps under the heading '### Priority Interventions'."

#### 2. Prompt Battle Ledger

| Version | Prompt Modifier Added | Output Quality Reflection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| V1 | "Write a tourism plan for Samal." | Too broad. Generated generic tourism advice and included irrelevant international examples. |
| V2 | Added regional advisor persona and specified Samal Island context. | Better localization, but recommendations remained too general for LGU implementation. |
| V3 | Added word limit, local infrastructure focus, and formatting constraints. | Produced focused, actionable, and highly localized recommendations suitable for stakeholders. |

## **AI Study Tools & Platforms (Content Critique)**
#### Topic: Agricultural Productivity Challenges and Climate Resilience in Mindanao

#### 1. AI-Generated Summary Audit
I prompted an AI discovery tool to summarize recent literature on agricultural productivity and climate adaptation in Mindanao. Below is the verification tracking matrix:

| AI-Generated Statement / Citation | Source Vetted Against | Status | Human Correction / Empirical Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Rice production in Mindanao declined by 50% during the 2023 El Niño period." | Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Crops Production Reports | ❌ Hallucination | Although drought conditions affected several provinces, official reports do not indicate a 50% decline. AI overstated the magnitude of losses. |
| "Climate-smart agriculture programs have been promoted to improve the resilience of smallholder farmers." | Department of Agriculture (DA) Climate Resilient Agriculture Program |  Verified | Confirmed. Government initiatives encourage water-efficient technologies and diversified cropping systems to strengthen adaptation. |

#### 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations
"While AI efficiently summarized multiple studies, some claims were exaggerated or unsupported. Verification with PSA, DA, and NEDA reports showed that AI may overstate impacts or confuse goals with actual outcomes. Thus, human validation remains essential for maintaining research accuracy and credibility."

AI Cleaning Instruction: "Scan the dataset. Identify all null cells in the 'Waste Generated' column and replace them using the median value for each city. Standardize all measurements into Metric Tons (MT) and remove duplicate records. Output the first 5 rows of the cleaned dataset."
