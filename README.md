### The Samal Eco-Tourism Prompt System

#### 1. System Prompt Template (V3 - Final Optimized)
"Act as a Senior Eco-Tourism Development Advisor specializing in the Island Garden City of Samal (IGaCoS), Davao del Norte. Your objective is to draft a 300-word implementation brief for local tourism stakeholders and LGU officials.

Context: Community-based tourism operators and coastal barangays are experiencing fluctuating visitor arrivals and increasing environmental pressures on marine and beach destinations.

Constraints: Use a professional, community-centered tone. Focus entirely on Samal Island’s tourism assets, ferry access routes, marine conservation areas, and local livelihood opportunities. Do Not reference foreign tourism models or international sustainability rankings. Avoid corporate jargon.

Format: Output in clear Markdown with exactly three actionable steps under the heading '### Priority Interventions'."

#### 1. AI-Generated Summary Audit
I prompted an AI discovery tool to summarize recent literature on agricultural productivity and climate adaptation in Mindanao. Below is the verification tracking matrix:

| AI-Generated Statement / Citation | Source Vetted Against | Status | Human Correction / Empirical Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Rice production in Mindanao declined by 50% during the 2023 El Niño period." | Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Crops Production Reports | ❌ Hallucination | Although drought conditions affected several provinces, official reports do not indicate a 50% decline. | "Climate-smart agriculture programs have been promoted to improve the resilience of smallholder farmers." | Department of Agriculture (DA) Climate Resilient Agriculture Program |  Verified | Confirmed. Government initiatives encourage water-efficient technologies and diversified cropping systems to strengthen adaptation. |

#### 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations
"While AI efficiently summarized multiple studies, some claims were exaggerated or unsupported. Thus, human validation remains essential for maintaining research accuracy and credibility."
